Our Story

Every pair has a soul.

Souls by Zamani is a small, independent atelier making shoes for men and women, plus bags and leather accessories for women, entirely by hand.

Where it started

A craft learned young

The story of Souls by Zamani begins in secondary school, in a workshop class where a teenager was handed a piece of leather, an awl and a length of waxed thread. That first pair was far from perfect, but the feeling of making something you could actually walk in never left.

Life moved on, but the craft stayed close. Now Zamani is going back to the bench: relearning the foundations properly, studying pattern cutting, lasting and finishing, and adding new skills like bag-making and small leather goods. This shop is where that work lives.

The name says it all. The sole is the foundation of every shoe; the soul is what the maker puts into it. We want everything we make to carry both.

What we believe

Made by hand

No production line. Every piece is patterned, cut, stitched and finished by hand, so small variations are marks of the maker, not flaws.

Made to last

We choose leathers that age beautifully and build shoes that can be re-soled. A pair from us should get better with time, not wear out.

Always learning

Craft is never finished. We keep studying, from traditional techniques to modern comfort, and every lesson ends up in the next pair.

The workshop

From hide to heel

Pattern

Designing on the last

Each style starts on a wooden last, the foot-shaped form that decides how a shoe fits. We tape it, draw the design and cut paper patterns from it.

Clicking

Cutting the leather

Leather is laid out and cut by hand. Every hide is different, so we read it for strength and grain, and put the best parts where they show and flex most.

Closing

Stitching the upper

The pieces are skived thin at the edges, then stitched together with the lining into the upper.

Lasting

Giving it shape

The upper is pulled tight over the last and left to set, so the shoe takes its final form.

Finishing

Sole, edges & polish

Soles are attached, edges trimmed and burnished, and the leather hand-polished. Each pair is inspected before it's boxed for you.
