Size guide

We make shoes in EU sizes. Stand on a sheet of paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance. Choose the size whose foot length is equal to or just above yours. Between sizes, or with a wide foot? Size up, or order made-to-measure.

Leather care

  • Rest them: give leather shoes a day off between wears so moisture can dry out.
  • Shoe trees: cedar shoe trees keep their shape and absorb moisture.
  • Clean & condition: brush off dust, wipe with a damp cloth, and condition every 2–3 months with a neutral leather cream.
  • Suede: use a suede brush and protector spray. Never use cream on suede.
  • Bags: stuff with paper to keep the shape and store in the dust bag, away from sunlight.
  • Resoling: our leather-soled shoes can be resoled. Send them back to us when the time comes.

Frequently asked questions

Are your products really handmade?

Yes. Every pair of shoes, every bag and every accessory is patterned, cut, stitched and finished by hand in our Lagos workshop.

Do you make kids' shoes?

Not at the moment. We make shoes for men and women, and bags and accessories for women.

Can I see the products in person?

Yes. You can visit the workshop by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book a time.

How do I pay?

Pay by card, bank transfer or USSD at checkout, by bank transfer, on delivery in Lagos, or by placing your order on WhatsApp.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

Yes, we ship worldwide with DHL in 5–10 working days after your order is made. Import duties may apply in your country.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Because we make to order, changes are possible until we start cutting your leather, usually within 24 hours. Contact us as soon as possible.

Do you do wholesale or stock other stores?

We're open to working with boutiques. Contact us with "Wholesale" as the topic.
