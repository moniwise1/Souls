Our Story

A craft learned young, now made new.

Souls by Zamani started at a secondary school workbench: an awl, waxed thread, and a stubborn wish to make something that lasts. Years later, Zamani is back at the bench, relearning the old techniques, studying new ones, and putting all of it into every pair.

We make shoes for men and women, and bags and small leather goods for women. Each piece passes through the same hands, from pattern to final polish.

— Zamani

From hide to heel

How every pair is made

Pattern

Each style starts as a paper pattern drafted over the last, the wooden foot form that gives a shoe its fit.

Clicking

Leather is hand-selected and cut piece by piece, reading the hide for its strongest, most even parts.

Lasting & stitching

Uppers are pulled tight over the last, then stitched to the sole for a structure that holds its shape.

Finishing

Edges are burnished, leather is hand-polished, and every pair is checked before it's boxed for you.
